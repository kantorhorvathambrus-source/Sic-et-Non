## What "corroborated" rests on

`corroborated` means the wording was confirmed without the source being read.
That covers a wide range, and the range is the point: several agreeing hits can
be one upstream copied four times. Classified by what the cited source is.

| Source class | Quotations | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| primary text, freely published | 28 | The text itself is online and the wording is stable across many independent printings. |
| author's own site or paper | 30 | Hosted by the author or by a course page reproducing their text. |
| journal, encyclopedia or scholarly index | 30 | A source with its own editorial standard, quoting the wording. |
| publisher, institution or organisation page | 44 | An institutional page carrying the wording; a single upstream in most cases. |
| quotation aggregator only | 8 | THE WEAK CLASS. Aggregators copy from each other, so several agreeing hits can be one upstream. Every quotation here is named below so it can be pulled first. |
